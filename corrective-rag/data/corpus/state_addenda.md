# State Addenda (synthetic)

State rules override the national policy whenever they are stricter. Look up the addendum by two-letter
state code. Where a state is silent, the national policy applies.

## CA - California
- Maximum APR on personal loans under $10,000: 30.00%.
- Loan documents must be offered in Spanish on request.
- Notice of a pending late fee must be mailed at least 10 days before the fee is assessed.
- HELOC right of rescission: 3 business days.

## TX - Texas
- Home equity combined LTV may not exceed 80%, which is stricter than the national 85% cap.
- A home equity loan may not close until 12 days after the application is received.
- Collections calls are limited to 7 attempts per 7-day period.
- Maximum APR on personal loans: 28.00%.

## NY - New York
- Civil usury cap on personal loans: 25.00% APR. Criminal usury cap: 36.00% APR; the lender never prices above 25.00%.
- Collections contact is allowed only between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM in the borrower's local time.
- Written notice is required before reporting a delinquency to a credit bureau.
- HELOC right of rescission: 3 business days.

## FL - Florida
- Documentary stamp tax of 0.35% applies to the promissory note on loans secured by real property.
- Maximum APR on personal loans: 30.00%.
- Auto loan repossession notice: 10 days.

## IL - Illinois
- Voluntary cooling-off period of 2 business days on auto loans; the borrower may return the vehicle and cancel.
- Maximum APR on personal loans: 36.00%, but the lender caps its own pricing at the national 23.99%.
- Small business loans under $50,000 require a plain-language cost disclosure.

## OH - Ohio
- Maximum APR on personal loans: 28.00%.
- Auto loan repossession notice: 20 days.
- Military borrowers covered by SCRA receive a 6% interest-rate cap on pre-service debt.

## Cooling-off summary
- Auto loan cooling-off exists only in IL (2 business days).
- HELOC rescission is 3 business days in every state, including CA and NY.
- No cooling-off period applies to personal loans in any state.
