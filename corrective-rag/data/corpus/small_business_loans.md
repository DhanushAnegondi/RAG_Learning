# Small Business Loans (synthetic)

Term loans for established small businesses. Pricing and underwriting differ from consumer products.

## Eligibility
- The business must have operated for at least 2 years.
- The principal owner must have a FICO score of at least 680.
- Minimum annual revenue is $120,000.
- Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) must be at least 1.25 after the new loan payment.

## Amounts, terms, and pricing
- Loan amounts range from $10,000 to $250,000.
- Terms are 12 to 84 months.
- Fixed APR ranges from 9.50% to 24.50% based on credit tier and time in business.
- Origination fee: 3% of the loan amount.
- A 2% prepayment fee applies if the loan is paid off within the first 12 months (`FEE-PREPAY-05`).

## Required documents
- Two years of business tax returns and a year-to-date profit-and-loss statement.
- Three months of business bank statements.
- A personal guarantee on form `BIZ-GUA-12` from every owner holding 20% or more of the company.
- Proof of business registration and a government-issued ID for each guarantor.

## Collateral
- Loans over $100,000 are secured by a blanket lien filed with a UCC-1 financing statement.
- Loans of $100,000 or less are unsecured but backed by the personal guarantee.

## Servicing
- Payments are collected by ACH on the due date.
- The grace period before a late fee is 5 days, and the late fee is 5% of the past-due amount (`FEE-LATE-04`).
- A loan 90 days past due is classified seriously delinquent and the guarantor is notified in writing.

## Use of funds
- Permitted uses include equipment, inventory, working capital, refinancing business debt, and tenant improvements.
- Funds may not be used for personal expenses, speculative investment, or paying owner distributions.
- Loans above $100,000 require a use-of-funds statement signed by the principal owner.

## Renewal and increases
- A borrower with 12 on-time payments may request a renewal, which is underwritten as a new application.
- A renewal that pays off the old loan within 12 months of origination still triggers `FEE-PREPAY-05`.
- Line increases on an existing loan are not offered; a new loan must be requested.

## Decline reasons
- Common reasons include DSCR below 1.25, a recent bankruptcy by any guarantor, and unresolved tax liens.
- An adverse action notice is still sent within 30 days of the decision.
