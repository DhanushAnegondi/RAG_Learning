# Forms and Disclosures Index (synthetic)

Each form has a code. Always cite the code, never the form title alone, when asking a customer to sign.

## Application and disclosure forms
- `DSC-114` - Truth-in-lending disclosure for installment loans (personal, auto, student refinance). Delivered
  before the borrower signs the note.
- `DSC-115` - Truth-in-lending disclosure for credit cards. Do not use it for installment loans.
- `ESI-001` - Electronic signature and electronic delivery consent.
- `DOC-INC-5` - Income verification worksheet for self-employed applicants.
- `CCS-310` - Credit card cardholder agreement.

## Decision forms
- `ADV-ACT-3` - Adverse action notice. Sent within 30 days of a declined application and must list the primary
  reasons and the credit score used.
- `ADV-ACT-4` - Adverse action notice for small business loans, sent to the business and each guarantor.
- `CNT-OFR-2` - Counteroffer notice, sent when the lender approves at different terms than requested.

## Servicing forms
- `PAY-ACH-9` - ACH authorization for automatic payments. The borrower may revoke it with 3 business days' notice.
- `HLP-220` - Hardship application, used for deferral, disaster forbearance, and temporary rate reduction requests.
- `PAY-PLN-6` - Payment plan agreement for delinquent accounts.

## Fraud and secured-lending forms
- `FRD-207` - Identity theft affidavit.
- `RSC-3` - Notice of right to rescind, delivered at HELOC closing. The rescission window is 3 business days.
- `BIZ-GUA-12` - Personal guarantee for small business loans.
- `UCC-001-B` - Internal checklist for UCC-1 lien filings on business loans.

## Delivery rules
- Disclosures must be delivered before the borrower signs, either on paper or by e-delivery after `ESI-001` is on file.
- A borrower who withdraws e-delivery consent receives paper copies within 5 business days at no charge.
- Spanish versions of each form carry the suffix `-ES`, for example `DSC-114-ES`.
- Superseded form versions are kept in the vault but marked inactive and never sent to new customers.

## Common mix-ups
- `DSC-114` and `DSC-115` look alike but are for different products: installment loans and credit cards.
- `ADV-ACT-3` is for consumer applications; `ADV-ACT-4` is for business applications.
- `HLP-220` starts a hardship request; `PAY-PLN-6` records the resulting payment plan.

## Retention
- Signed forms are retained for 5 years after the account closes.
- Declined applications and their adverse action notices are retained for 25 months.
- Forms are stored in the document vault; paper originals are scanned within 2 business days.
