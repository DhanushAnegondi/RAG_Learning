# Hardship and Collections Procedures (synthetic)

This document extends the servicing runbook. Delinquency stages and the one-time deferral rule in the
runbook remain in force.

## Hardship programs
- Payment deferral: up to two payments per 12-month period, added to the end of the term (see runbook).
- Disaster forbearance: after a federally declared disaster, borrowers in the affected area may pause
  payments for up to 90 days. Late fees are suspended and no negative credit reporting occurs during the pause.
- Temporary rate reduction: borrowers with a documented income loss may request a reduced rate for up to
  6 months. The reduced rate is never below 5.00% APR.
- Hardship requests are submitted on form `HLP-220` with proof of the change in circumstances.

## Contact rules
- Collections contact is limited to 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM in the borrower's local time.
- No more than 7 collection calls may be placed to a borrower in any 7-day period.
- A written request to stop contact must be honored, except for legally required notices.
- A debt validation notice is sent within 5 days of the first collections contact.

## Settlements
- A settlement of less than the full balance needs approval from a collections manager when the balance exceeds $5,000.
- Settlement offers are valid for 14 days and must be confirmed in writing.
- Settled accounts are reported as settled for less than the full balance.

## Escalation after charge-off
- Accounts are evaluated for charge-off at 120 days past due, as stated in the runbook.
- Charged-off accounts are placed with an outside recovery agency after 180 days past due.
- Recovery agencies must follow the same contact rules as internal collections.

## Military borrowers
- Borrowers covered by SCRA receive a 6% interest-rate cap on debt taken before active duty.
- Repossession and foreclosure actions against covered borrowers require court approval.

## Bankruptcy
- On notice of a bankruptcy filing, all collection activity stops immediately and the account is
  flagged for the legal team.
- A bankruptcy discharged within the last 24 months makes a new applicant ineligible for a personal loan.
