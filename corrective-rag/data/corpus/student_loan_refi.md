# Student Loan Refinance (synthetic)

Refinance replaces existing private or federal student loans with one new fixed or variable rate loan.

## Eligibility
- Minimum FICO score is 680, higher than the personal loan minimum of 660.
- Maximum DTI is 40%, lower than the 43% personal loan limit.
- Applicant must have earned a degree from an accredited school and have at least $10,000 of qualifying debt.
- A cosigner may be added to meet the credit or income requirements.

## Amounts, terms, and rates
- Refinance amounts range from $10,000 to $200,000.
- Terms are 5, 10, 15, or 20 years.
- Fixed APR ranges from 4.99% to 11.49%; variable APR ranges from 4.49% to 10.99% and is indexed to SOFR.
- Autopay earns a 0.25% rate discount.
- No origination fee (`FEE-ORIG-02`) and no prepayment penalty.

## Federal loan warning
- Refinancing federal loans permanently forfeits federal protections, including income-driven repayment plans
  and Public Service Loan Forgiveness eligibility.
- The borrower must sign an acknowledgment of this loss before funding.

## Cosigner release
- A cosigner may be released after 24 consecutive on-time payments if the primary borrower meets current
  credit and income standards on their own.

## Forbearance
- Student refinance borrowers may request forbearance of up to 12 months in total over the life of the loan.
- Interest continues to accrue during forbearance. This is separate from the personal loan payment deferral
  of up to two payments per 12-month period.

## Application process
- Applicants upload the most recent statement for each loan to be refinanced and a recent pay stub.
- The lender pays the old servicers directly; the borrower keeps paying the old loans until payoff is confirmed.
- Pre-qualification uses a soft credit pull and does not affect the credit score.
- A final decision is normally returned within 3 business days of a complete application.

## Disability and death
- The refinance loan is discharged if the primary borrower dies; a cosigner is not responsible for the balance.
- Total and permanent disability discharge requires a physician certification and review by the servicing team.

## In-school and residency
- Medical and dental residents may make interest-only payments of $100 per month for up to 48 months.
- Borrowers still enrolled in school are not eligible for refinance.
