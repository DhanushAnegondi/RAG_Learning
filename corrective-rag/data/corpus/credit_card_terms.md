# Credit Card Terms (synthetic)

The lender offers two consumer cards: Everyday Card and Rewards Card. Terms below apply to both unless noted.

## Eligibility
- Cardholders must be 18 or older and a legal resident.
- Minimum FICO score for a credit card is 640. This is lower than the 660 required for an unsecured personal
  loan because card exposure is smaller and revolving.
- Maximum DTI for card approval is 45%, measured without the new card payment.

## Rates and credit limits
- Purchase APR ranges from 19.99% to 29.99% based on credit tier.
- Penalty APR is 29.99%, applied after a payment is 60 days late and reviewed after six on-time payments.
- Credit limits start at $500 and may reach $25,000.
- Cash advance APR is 29.99% with no grace period.

## Fees
- Annual fee: $0 for the Everyday Card and $95 for the Rewards Card.
- Balance transfer fee: 3% of the transferred amount, with a 0% promotional APR for 15 months.
- Cash advance fee: 5% of the amount or $10, whichever is greater.
- Late and returned payment fees are listed under `FEE-LATE-03` and `FEE-NSF-01`.

## Grace period and minimum payment
- New purchases have a 21-day grace period from the statement date when the previous balance was paid in full.
- Minimum payment is the greater of $35 or 1% of the statement balance plus interest and fees.
- Statements are issued monthly; autopay can be set for the minimum, a fixed amount, or the full balance.

## Rewards
- The Rewards Card earns 1.5% cash back on all purchases and 3% on groceries up to $6,000 per year.
- Cash back posts after the statement closes and expires after 24 months of account inactivity.

## Credit limit changes
- Limit increase requests are reviewed no more than once every 6 months and may use a soft credit pull.
- Automatic limit increases are never applied to an account that was late in the last 12 months.
- A limit decrease requires 45 days of advance notice unless the account is 60 days past due.

## Account closure
- A cardholder may close the account at any time by written or online request; the balance remains due.
- The lender may close an account after 24 months of inactivity, with notice, and reports it as closed by the lender.
- Unredeemed rewards are forfeited on closure by the lender for cause.

## Disputes and liability
- Billing errors must be reported within 60 days of the statement date.
- Cardholder liability for unauthorized use is capped at $50. See the fraud and identity document.
- The cardholder agreement is form `CCS-310`.
