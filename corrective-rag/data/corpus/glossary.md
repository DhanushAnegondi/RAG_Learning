# Lending Glossary (synthetic)

Plain-language definitions of the acronyms used across the policy documents.

## Ratios
- **DTI (debt-to-income ratio):** total monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income. A lower DTI
  means more room in the borrower's budget.
- **LTV (loan-to-value ratio):** the loan balance divided by the appraised value of the asset that secures it,
  such as a vehicle or a home.
- **CLTV (combined loan-to-value ratio):** all loans secured by the same property, including the new line of
  credit, divided by the property value. HELOC limits are expressed as CLTV.
- **DSCR (debt service coverage ratio):** a business's net operating income divided by its yearly debt payments.

## Pricing terms
- **APR (annual percentage rate):** the yearly cost of borrowing, including fees, expressed as a percentage.
- **APY (annual percentage yield):** the yearly return on a deposit including compounding. It is not used for loans.
- **SOFR:** a benchmark interest rate used to index some variable-rate loans.
- **Prime rate:** a benchmark bank lending rate used to index HELOC variable rates.
- **Penalty APR:** a higher rate applied after a serious late payment.

## Credit and reporting
- **FICO score:** a credit score from 300 to 850 used to rank applicants by repayment risk.
- **Charge-off:** an accounting step in which the lender writes the balance off as a loss. The borrower still owes the debt.
- **Seriously delinquent:** a loan 90 or more days past due.
- **Adverse action:** a decline, or a less favorable offer, based on information in the credit file.

## Payments
- **NSF (non-sufficient funds):** a payment that is returned because the account lacked the money.
- **ACH (Automated Clearing House):** the bank network used for electronic debits and credits.
- **Deferral:** moving missed payments to the end of the term. Different from forbearance, which pauses payments.
- **Forbearance:** a temporary pause or reduction in payments, with interest still accruing.

## Legal and regulatory
- **TILA (Truth in Lending Act):** the law requiring clear disclosure of loan costs.
- **ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act):** the law that prohibits discrimination in credit decisions.
- **FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act):** the law governing credit reports and adverse action notices.
- **SCRA (Servicemembers Civil Relief Act):** the law giving active-duty military borrowers rate and court protections.
- **UCC-1:** a financing statement filed to record a lender's claim on business assets.
- **Usury:** charging an interest rate above the legal maximum.
