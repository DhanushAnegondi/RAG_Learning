# Fraud and Identity Verification (synthetic)

## Identity verification at application
- Every applicant must present a government-issued photo ID and a Social Security number that matches
  the credit file.
- Name, date of birth, and address are checked against the credit bureau header data.
- Applicants who cannot be verified automatically are routed to manual review, which must finish within 3 business days.

## Red flags
- A mismatch between the application address and the credit file address.
- A phone number or email address first seen on several unrelated applications.
- An initial fraud alert or credit freeze on the applicant's file. An initial fraud alert means the lender
  must take extra steps to contact the applicant before approval.
- Documents with inconsistent fonts or metadata.

## Account access security
- One-time passcodes sent by text or email expire after 10 minutes.
- An online account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlocked only after identity re-verification.
- A password change triggers a notification to the registered email address.

## Unauthorized transactions
- Credit card holders are liable for at most $50 of unauthorized charges, and usually nothing if the
  loss is reported promptly.
- Unauthorized ACH debits must be reported within 60 days of the statement showing the debit.
- Card holders should report a lost or stolen card at once; a replacement is mailed within 3 business days.

## Identity theft claims
- A victim of identity theft completes the identity theft affidavit, form `FRD-207`, and a police report if available.
- The lender acknowledges a fraud claim within 30 days and completes the investigation within 90 days.
- While an identity theft claim is open, collections activity on the disputed account is suspended and
  the account is not reported as delinquent.

## Suspicious activity handling
- Employees who suspect fraud escalate to the fraud operations queue and never tip off the customer.
- Internal detection thresholds and scoring rules are confidential and are not part of this document.
