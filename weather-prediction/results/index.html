sprint-01: forecast error audit and 7/30-day outlook (bengaluru)

Measure how far published forecasts drift from what happened, then forecast the next week (published forecast, corrected only where CV shows it helps) and next month (climatology + decaying anomaly), each with a measured p10-p90 range.

What the run found

  • Published day-ahead max-temp forecasts for bengaluru (2022-01-01 to 2026-11-13, 1757 days) missed by 0.965 C on average; 12% of days were off by 2 C or more and 3% by 3 C or more (worst 4.9 C).
  • Forecasts issued 7 days ahead missed by 1.548 C on average; 12% of days were off by 3 C or more.
  • Bias: published max temps run +0.11 C at lead 1 over the whole period (positive = forecast too warm); it drifts by year as the upstream model changes.
  • Test window (2025-11-14 onward), max temp: kept the published forecast at lead 1, 2, 3, 4 (no correction beat it in CV); correction lowered error at lead 5, 6, 7; no lead got worse. The p10-p90 range held the actual value on 61.6% to 81.4% of days (target 80%).
  • min temp: kept the published forecast at lead 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (no correction beat it in CV); correction lowered error at no lead; lead 7 got worse: 0.85 -> 1.41 C (-66.5%). The p10-p90 range held the actual value on 64.4% to 95.9% of days (target 80%).
  • Beyond a week: max temp lead 30: model 1.39 C vs climatology 1.45 C vs persistence 2.21 C; min temp lead 30: model 0.95 C vs climatology 0.98 C vs persistence 1.47 C.

  • Actuals are ERA5 reanalysis for the grid cell, not a weather station; the last few days of the archive are preliminary.

  • Leads 8-30 have no day-level skill in any public model; the 30-day line is a seasonal expectation with a measured range, not a weather forecast.
  • One test window of 12 months; MAE differences under about 0.1 C are within noise.
  • Training rows are cut on target date, not issue date: up to 6 days of actuals just before the test window were not yet known when a lead-7 forecast was issued (about 0.1% of rows).
  • Temperature only. Rain is fetched but not modelled this sprint.
